YouthMend

Youth mental health platform providing early intervention and support for schools and families.

Who this is for

Schools Parents & guardians Pediatricians Counselors

Where we are

YouthMend is early-stage. We're building toward early-intervention support for schools and families, starting with tools that keep grown-ups in a child's life equipped to help — not tools that diagnose or treat a child directly. Individual clinical care always stays with licensed counselors and providers.
